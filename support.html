Subscriptions

Manage or cancel Taskling Plus anytime in your iPhone’s Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.

Moved to a new iPhone? Open Taskling › Settings › Restore purchases.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your account and data

Delete your account and all your data anytime in Taskling › Settings › Account & data › Delete account.

Using Taskling without an account? Your plans stay on your iPhone only.

Taskling

Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Taskling is a calm planner for every kind of brain. We built it to keep as much as possible on your iPhone. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

We don’t sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

Voice is turned into text on your iPhone. Taskling’s AI features are off unless you turn them on.

You can use Taskling without an account. If you create one, you can delete it and all your data from Settings at any time.

What we collect

Account details, if you create an account: your email address, and your name if you give it. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple shares a user identifier and, if you allow it, your name and email (which can be a private relay address).

What you put in the app: tasks, sub-tasks, notes, tags, routines, focus sessions, mood check-ins and reflections, profiles, and co-planner chats.

Question of the day answers: if you post an answer, it is visible to other signed-in Taskling users along with the name you chose.

Settings such as theme, font, sounds and reminder times. These stay on your device.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

Voice and microphone

When you tap the microphone, Taskling listens only until you stop or pause. Audio is not stored.

Your speech is converted to text by Apple’s speech recognition on your iPhone. If Apple’s on-device model isn’t available, Apple may process the audio on its servers under Apple’s own privacy policy. Taskling can also use an open-source speech model (Whisper) that runs entirely on your iPhone; it downloads once from a model host and then works offline. Your audio is never sent to us.

AI features (off by default)

Taskling works without AI. If you turn on “Use AI” in Settings, the text you send to Sprig and a short summary of today’s tasks are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to write a reply. This data is used only to provide the feature. You can turn it off at any time.

How we use your information

To run Taskling: save and sync your plans, sign you in, send the reminders you ask for, and show your progress.

To keep Taskling safe: prevent abuse and fix problems.

To contact you about your account, or with tips and updates only if you opted in. You can opt out at any time.

Where your data is stored

Without an account, everything stays on your device.

With an account, your data is stored with our database and sign-in provider (Supabase) and protected so only your account can read it. Data may be processed in countries other than yours; we use providers with appropriate safeguards.

Who we share it with

Service providers that help us run Taskling: Supabase (hosting, database, sign-in), Apple (Sign in with Apple, speech recognition), the model host the on-device speech model is downloaded from, and Anthropic only if you turn AI on.

Authorities, if the law requires it.

We never sell or rent your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your data from our systems promptly, except where we must keep something by law. Local data is removed when you erase it in Settings or delete the app.

Your rights and choices

You can view and edit your data in the app, export it by contacting us, and delete your account and data from Settings → Account → Delete account.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your data, and to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us and we’ll help.

You can turn off notifications, the microphone and speech recognition in iPhone Settings at any time.

Mood and wellbeing information

Mood check-ins and reflections may be sensitive. We use them only to show your own insights inside the app. Taskling is not a medical service and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Children

Taskling is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and per-user data rules. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your device locked.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we’ll update the date above and let you know in the app.
